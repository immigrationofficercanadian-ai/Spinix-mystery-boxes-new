Questions & answers

What is SPINIX?

A mystery-box concept with five box levels and a planned Friday grand draw. This is a preview website.

When is the grand draw?

The planned draw day is every Friday. The official time, entry cut-off and rules must be published before launch.

Can I pay here now?

No. This preview does not take payments or create entries. A live service needs the correct Kenyan approval and secure payment checks.

Will every box win?

The final prize rules and chances are not set. They must be clearly explained before paid entries are offered.

Where can I see winners?

Verified winners will appear in the SPINIX Winners section after a real draw and permission to publish their details.
