What is SPINIX Mystery Boxes?

SPINIX is a mystery-box concept. This website is currently a preview prototype, not a live paid service.

Can I make a payment here?

No. Payments and paid box openings are disabled. A live service needs regulatory approval, secure payment processing and tested backend verification.

Does every box contain a prize?

The live product rules are not yet established. Before launch, accurate prize conditions, winning probabilities where required, eligibility rules and non-winning outcomes must be published.

How are winners selected?

No live winner-selection system is active. Any future selection process must be fair, auditable, secure and consistent with approved rules and applicable law.

How do I receive a reward?

Reward delivery procedures, timelines, identity checks and support arrangements must be published before launch.
